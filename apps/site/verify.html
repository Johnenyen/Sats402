Verification

Check anything yourself.

This page only reads the Tachi daemon: no keys, no wallet, no signing. A settlement record is daemon-returned and re-fetchable, which is exactly what makes it a receipt.

No tx id handy? Verify a settlement from the recorded run.

Verify a full payment payload

For developers: paste the PaymentPayload a client sent and every check runs live — the signature over the full challenge, the settlement re-fetch, and the committed record with payee amount and payer-owned inputs.
