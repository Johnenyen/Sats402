How the agent works

    

      The interesting part is step 2: the agent decides, on its own, whether it
      is allowed to pay.
    

    

      
1
Challenge. The service answers 402 with its price and payment terms.

      
2
Policy. Per-call cap, session budget, payee allowlist. A refusal happens here, locally, before any transaction exists.

      
3
Settlement. The agent signs and broadcasts its own tachi_tx for exactly the price asked. Its key, its money, its signature.

      
4
Proof and retry. The retry carries the settlement id. The service verifies it on-chain and serves the product.
