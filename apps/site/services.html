Grounded AI completion

50 sats / call

A model call that is grounded in live data it bought itself. This service is an agent with its own key and its own spend policy: it cannot answer until it has paid the network data service for the facts the answer needs. One request, two purchase types. 

POST https://sats402.vercel.app/api/services/inference
{ "question": "What is the recommended fee right now?" }

  }

Calling any endpoint

Identical for all four. Any x402-style client can do this.

1

Call the endpoint. Read the price and the challenge from PAYMENT-REQUIRED.

2

Pay exactly the price: your agent signs and broadcasts its own tachi_tx to the payee in the challenge.

3

Retry with the proof in PAYMENT-SIGNATURE. The response arrives with PAYMENT-RESPONSE carrying the settlement id.

Prefer the SDK: @sats402/agent does all three steps and enforces your spending policy. Services need only @sats402/express in front of their handler. Agents can discover these endpoints machine-readably: services.json lists every service with its price and category, and the full OpenAPI 3.1 spec describes the contracts. The paywall guards rendered content too: GET /api/protected (5 sats per view; an agent receives the 402 challenge, settles on Tachi, and retries with PAYMENT-SIGNATURE to receive the HTML page, or run node packages/cli/bin/sats402.mjs verify <txid> to verify).

How the agent works

The interesting part is step 2: the agent decides, on its own, whether it is allowed to pay.

1

Challenge. The service answers 402 with its price and payment terms.

2

Policy. Per-call cap, session budget, payee allowlist. A refusal happens here, locally, before any transaction exists.

3

Settlement. The agent signs and broadcasts its own tachi_tx for exactly the price asked. Its key, its money, its signature.

4

Proof and retry. The retry carries the settlement id. The service verifies it on-chain and serves the product.
